What it is

  • One figure — what you can spend — and where it sits.
  • One list of things only you can clear: a bill to book, a budget just gone over, a variable rule waiting for a figure, a trip that started.
  • The month collapses to one sentence plus links.

Why

  • Nothing here disagrees with a month picker, because there is no month picker.
  • Every row is an action, so the screen is empty on a good day — which is itself the answer.
  • It stops repeating Money and Goals.

What it costs

  • You lose browsing past months here — it moves to Money and Budgets.
  • No breakdown, no trend, no recent list. If you like reading those over coffee, this is the wrong one.
